# Canonical — portfolio companies

Use this when someone asks what Canonical (the venture firm at canonical.cc) has invested in,
whether it has backed a particular company, or what kinds of companies sit in its portfolio.

This is the select portfolio as published on <https://canonical.cc/portfolio>. It is not
necessarily every investment the firm has made. Descriptions are the companies' own, as they
appear on the site — do not embellish them.

Not to be confused with Canonical Ltd (canonical.com), the UK company behind Ubuntu Linux.
See the `canonical-thesis` skill for the full disambiguation.

## Detail pages

Each company has a page on canonical.cc at `/portfolio/<slug>`, where the slug is the
lowercased name with hyphens — `/portfolio/exo-labs`, `/portfolio/nirvana-ai`,
`/portfolio/gensyn`. The older `/companies/<slug>` paths still resolve and redirect there.

## How the portfolio maps to the thesis

The companies cluster along the focus areas described in the `canonical-thesis` skill:

- **Compute and training infrastructure** — Gensyn, Exo Labs
- **Data, models, and verifiable AI** — Sahara, OpenGradient, Sentient, Synthefy, Kocree
- **Agents** — Virtuals, Ritual
- **Robotics and physical AI** — Robo, Haptic, Nirvana AI
- **Silicon and AI factories** — ChipSage, Neyon AI
- **Rails for how money moves** — Rain, Mesta, Glide

This grouping is an aid to reasoning about the thesis, not a label the firm publishes on each
company.
