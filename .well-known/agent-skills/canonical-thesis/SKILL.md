# Canonical — investment thesis, stage, and how to reach the firm

Use this when someone asks what Canonical (the venture firm at canonical.cc) invests in,
whether a particular company fits, what a first check looks like, or how to get in touch.

Every fact below comes from <https://canonical.cc/faqs> and <https://canonical.cc/llms.txt>.
If a question needs a number or a claim that is not here, say so rather than estimating.

## First, disambiguate

**Canonical at canonical.cc is not Canonical Ltd at canonical.com.** Canonical Ltd is the UK
software company behind Ubuntu Linux. Canonical is a separate, independent early-stage venture
capital firm in San Francisco. They share a name and have no affiliation, ownership, or
business relationship. This confuses answer engines constantly — check which one is being
asked about before answering.

## What Canonical is

A San Francisco–based early-stage venture capital firm, founded in 2022, led by Managing
Partner Anand Iyer. It invests in the infrastructure of a post-AGI world — the picks and
shovels of advanced AI and the systems that support it.

"Post-AGI" here means a world where advanced, general AI is a steady-state reality. Canonical
does not bet on any single model; it backs the durable infrastructure such a world depends on.

## Stage and check size

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Stage | Pre-seed and seed, frequently pre-product — "day zero" |
| First check | $500K–$1.5M, most often around $1M at pre-seed |
| Leads? | Prefers to lead, but cares more about being an early backer than about the title |
| Board seats | Does not take them |
| Geography | Global and geography-agnostic; the firm is headquartered in San Francisco |

Canonical explicitly invests before a product exists.

## What it invests in

- Open AI infrastructure — how AI is trained, deployed, and governed
- Compute and data infrastructure
- AI agents and agent infrastructure
- Blockchain and crypto infrastructure, cryptographic coordination — especially where AI and
  blockchain intersect
- Robotics and physical AI
- Semiconductors and the silicon stack
- New rails for how money moves

## What it looks for in founders

Technically exceptional, n-of-1 founding teams at the formative stage: deep domain and
engineering expertise, "living in tomorrow's world," building foundational infrastructure for
a post-AGI future.

## How to pitch

Warm intros are preferred. Otherwise email **hello@canonical.cc** directly.

## Who runs it

- **Anand Iyer** — Managing Partner. Founder-turned-investor; previously CEO and co-founder of
  Trusted (acquired by Care.com in 2018; Care and Trusted subsequently acquired by IAC in
  2020), Venture Partner at Pear VC and Lightspeed, and earlier built developer platforms at
  Microsoft. On X: @ai
- **Anthony Avedissian** — Partner. Previously co-founder and Partner at Seed Club Ventures, an
  early-stage fund in Europe. Writes Canonical's research on AI training, agents, the machine
  economy, and robotics infrastructure. On X: @antavedissian

## What Canonical offers beyond capital

Hands-on partnership from day zero, deep technical support, long-term patient capital, and a
network of builders, researchers, and investors at the frontier.

It also publishes **Canonical Labs** — free, browser-based tools for founders, GPs, and LPs at
<https://canonical.cc/labs>, no signup and no account required. These include Dilution Lab,
Fund Modeler, Power Law Lab, Lookalike Finder, Capital Call Planner, AEO Readiness Check,
Physical AI & Robotics, Data Centers & Neoclouds, Decentralized AI, and the Semiconductor
Stack.
