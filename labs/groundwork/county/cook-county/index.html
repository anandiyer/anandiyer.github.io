[01] What is on file

15 permitted facilities.

These facilities hold active air permits in EPA's national registry. Groundwork has not yet read IL's permit documents, so generator counts and permitted emissions show as pending here — unlike Virginia, where every figure is read from the permit itself.

[02] What the permits actually authorise

What this does — and doesn't — mean.

It is smaller than it sounds

These are emergency backup generators. They are permitted to run during grid outages and for routine testing — typically tens of hours a year, not continuously. A permit is a ceiling on what a site may emit, not a measurement of what it does emit.

A permit also is not a building. An issued permit establishes approval; it does not establish that anything is built, energised or running.

None of the 15 sites here that Groundwork could locate sits in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area.

It is bigger than any one filing shows

No single permit discloses the total permitted in this county. That number exists only because 15 separately-published filings were added together. Each one, read alone, looks routine.
